01 / Who am I

Purpose driven.
People focused.
Always learning.

I am a Romanian-born educator working at the intersection of school leadership, learning design, professional learning, movement and experiential education. I believe learning becomes powerful when people can use it beyond the place where they learned it.

Today I am Middle Years Programme (MYP) Assistant Head and Physical and Health Education (PHE) Teacher at Albanian College Tirana, and a Professional Learning Coach and Facilitator at Education to Change the World, where I work alongside best-selling author Julie Stern. Together we co-designed the Physical Education and Health Learning that Transfers course and certified over 100 physical educators worldwide.

Beyond the classroom I explore the same ideas through Outdoor Joy, using movement, challenge, nature, reflection and connection as tools for learning and human development. The question behind everything I do:

How do we design experiences that build capabilities people can transfer to life?

04 / Learning that Transfers

Acquire.
Connect.
Transfer.

The ACT model, created by Julie Stern, Kayla Duncan and Trevor Aleo, turns PE lessons into understanding students carry for life: from organizing ideas, to connecting concepts, to transferring learning into real-life situations.

A / Acquire

Supporting students to organize pieces of information into sets of ideas.

C / Connect

Making connections and understanding conceptual relationships.

T / Transfer

Transferring new understanding into real-life situations, from similar to dissimilar contexts.

Join the PE and Health course Co-led with author and global consultant Julie Stern. Over 100 educators certified worldwide.
